## History waits to be rewritten. Make the throne yours.

[**Otenos**](https://otenos.com) is a free grand strategy MMO you play in your browser and on your phone, with nothing to download. Grow your holding, raise your army, clash with rivals or forge alliances. Trade sends your caravans down the roads, those who hold the ancient altars reap the bounty of the rites, and the legendary buildings go to the boldest bids at the auction table.

Every Age opens on a new map, everyone plays by the same rules, and on the final day the throne finds its ruler. Play in English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Português and Español.

**[Play free at otenos.com](https://otenos.com)**

### Open source

We share the tools we built while making the game. Each one guards Otenos in production and exists because a real bug reached our players first.

- [`@otenos/stylelint-media-order`](https://github.com/otenos/oss/tree/main/packages/stylelint-media-order): catches `@media` rules silently overridden by a later base rule
- [`@otenos/i18n-catalog-lint`](https://github.com/otenos/oss/tree/main/packages/i18n-catalog-lint): keeps translation catalogs in step: keys, placeholders, duplicates and vue-i18n syntax traps
